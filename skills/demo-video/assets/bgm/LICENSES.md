# BGM licenses

The catalog (`catalog.json`) is generated by `build-catalog.py` from Incompetech's public index. No audio is stored in this repository; `dv.py bgm use` downloads a track from the source at first use and verifies the sha256 recorded in the catalog (FR-072).

Tiers: `verified: "popularity"` tracks were selected from a source whose public use is documented (Wikipedia: featured in thousands of films and millions of YouTube videos) using the composer's own feel/tempo tags; nobody on the team listened to them. Set `verified: "listened"` only after a person has heard the whole track and confirmed mood, energy, density, no vocals and the loop seam.

Not bundled: Pixabay (no redistribution), FreePD (source gone), aggregator sites (license must be re-checked per track at the original source).
